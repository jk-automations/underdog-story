# Submission draft: Most Viral

**Status: draft for the founders. Not submitted, not posted.**

**Title:** Underdog Story: any small business, one underdog sitcom

**One line:** Pick a business, press Run, get a 105-second vertical underdog episode with voices, a score, captions and
an episode-2 tease, built from ComfyUI partner nodes in one App Mode app.

**Why it is made to travel**

- A proven arc, not a prompt: humiliation by a flashy chain, a low point everyone recognises (the car dies, no money for
  the fix), the heart (a family heirloom), a viral comeback the viewer watches happen (the view counter climbs), a
  comeuppance and an earned payoff, then a final joke. The bakery version (*The Last Croissant*, drafted in Google Flow
  with the same arc) is the one our founders called "best one yet".
- Built-in series hooks: every episode ends with a DM from a different kind of business ("Help. A MEGA-something just
  opened next to my taco truck."), so each film tees up the next one.
- Any business: barber shop, taco truck, nail salon and bakery are built in; Gemini fills the same beats for anything
  else. Local businesses share these to their own customers, which is where the views come from.
- Craft rules from our own editing notes are baked into every prompt: locked-off camera on the rival's landmark, moving
  background life, title cards on time jumps, the question before the punchline, no prop appearing from nowhere,
  dissolves that never cut a word, a score levelled and ducked under the dialogue, −14 LUFS master.

**What it uses:** Gemini 3.1 Pro (optional), Nano Banana 2, Veo 3.1 lite, Sonilo video-to-music, plus our own nodes for
the script checks, the cut and the mix. A free draft mode runs the whole pipeline with storyboard clips for $0.

**Cost:** about $7.60 per film on Veo 3.1 lite (price badges, ComfyUI 0.42). Free draft: $0.
