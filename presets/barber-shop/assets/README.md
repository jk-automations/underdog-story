# Barber shop: the character sheets to add here

The paid demo (`tools/run_paid_demo.py`) refuses to spend until all of these are present. Check with
`python3 tools/stage_preset.py --preset barber-shop --check`. Every sheet: PNG, at least 1024 px on the short side,
the same glossy 3D animated family-film style, fictional people only, no readable text except where noted, no real logos.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `owner.png` | The owner: the barber in his sixties with a grey pompadour, a neat white beard and a navy barber smock. Character sheet: front, three-quarter, side, two or three expressions (proud, tired, smug). |
| `rival.png` | The rival influencer: the young man with bleached spiky hair in a shiny silver tracksuit, holding a phone on a selfie stick. Front, three-quarter, side, a grin and a deflated look. |
| `regular.png` | The loyal regular: the retired mail carrier with a big grey moustache in a tan windbreaker. Front, three-quarter, side, warm smile. |
| `location.png` | The owner's place: the old corner barbershop with a red-and-white barber pole. One image with the street exterior and the interior (the vintage barber chair by the big mirror, the waiting bench, hot towels). No readable shop sign. |
| `rival_store.png` | The rival landmark across the street: a chrome mega-salon with a giant spinning razor on the roof and one sign that reads exactly `MEGAFADE`. Street-level, the whole storefront in frame. |

Hero 1 and hero 2 (the agency duo) are shared with the bakery sample: `../../small-business-rescue-bakery/assets/hero1.png`
and `hero2.png` are used automatically.
